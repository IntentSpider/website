Architecture Overview

IntentSpider Webnet introduces a novel paradigm in predictive text interfaces, focusing on textual tension and contextual fluid language modeling. By compiling our high-performance engine to WebAssembly (WASM), we bring unprecedented predictive capabilities directly into the browser, ensuring absolute privacy and zero server round-trips during typing.

The system continuously measures structural intent and semantic trajectory, updating probability distributions in real-time across the QWERTY layout.
