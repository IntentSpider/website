Heating the Graph (Initial Start)

When you start fresh with your own profile, the IntentSpider engine begins in a cold state. It relies on your typing cadence (speed and rhythm) to build up its internal valence and arousal signals. To "heat the graph", you simply need to answer about 30 to 40 questions naturally. As the system models your unique cadence, it will gain confidence and begin surfacing highly contextual predictions.
